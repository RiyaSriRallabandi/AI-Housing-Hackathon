CHAPTER 913
Use Exceptions and Conditions Not Listed in Use Table
§ 913.01 General.
The purpose of this chapter is to provide a complete listing and reference to all Administrator Exceptions, Special Exceptions and Conditional Uses which are not found within the Use Table of Chapter 911.
