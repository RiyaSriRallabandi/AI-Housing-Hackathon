            Data used for the Brookline example

            • City zoning GIS (WPRDC)
            • Pittsburgh Zoning Code, Title 9 (eCode360)
            • Census ACS 5-year and 2020 PL tables
            • HUD CHAS cost-burden (tract, not unit prices)
            • Allegheny County assessments and valid sales; ICC Building Valuation Data for construction cost direction
            • PRT stop locations; City FEMA 2026 flood overlay as a screen, not a determination
